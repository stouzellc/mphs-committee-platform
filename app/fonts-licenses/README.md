Source: fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com and github.com/google/fonts, downloaded 25 Oct 2026. The `.woff2` files are Google Fonts' own Latin subsets, served unchanged.

**Reserved Font Names.** Alfa Slab One and Graduate declare Reserved Font Names in their licenses. Under the OFL, a *modified* version may not use those names. These files are Google's distributed versions, unmodified by this project. If you ever subset or alter them yourself, rename the families in `public/css/fonts.css` and the files.
